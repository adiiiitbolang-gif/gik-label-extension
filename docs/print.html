Open this page from a print link in Lark (a batch or a packing).
    Open in Chrome to print
    Lark's built-in browser can't print. Tap "Open in Chrome to print" (or ⋯ → Open in browser), then press Print labels and choose your label printer.
    Send to printer app shares 50 × 30 mm label images to your thermal printer's app. Print labels uses the phone/PC print dialog (set paper 50 × 30 mm, margins none).
